# Traceable RAG Brand Assets

The production logo is a deterministic vector reconstruction of the selected
`B1 Continuous TR` concept. The mark combines a continuous `T`/`R` monogram
with a precise terminal cut representing trace-to-source verification.

## Assets

- `traceable-rag-mark.svg`: primary two-tone mark on transparent background.
- `traceable-rag-mark-mono.svg`: single-color mark for print and constrained surfaces.
- `traceable-rag-mark-reverse.svg`: white mark for dark backgrounds.
- `traceable-rag-lockup.svg`: primary horizontal logo without subtitle.
- `traceable-rag-lockup-mono.svg`: monochrome horizontal logo.
- `traceable-rag-lockup-reverse.svg`: white horizontal logo for dark backgrounds.
- `traceable-rag-lockup-tagline-en.svg`: horizontal logo with English subtitle.
- `traceable-rag-lockup-tagline-zh.svg`: horizontal logo with Chinese subtitle.
- `png/`: ready-to-use raster exports for common digital surfaces.

English subtitle: `Verifiable document retrieval for AI agents.`

Chinese subtitle: `面向 AI 智能体的可验证文档检索`

## Palette

- Ink: `#202033`
- Brand violet: `#6B65D8`
- Deep violet: `#514BB8`
- Muted text: `#6F7187`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Usage

- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor with gradients, add glow, or place the primary mark inside a decorative badge.
- Keep clear space of at least one stroke width around the mark.
- Use the simplified `/favicon.svg` application at browser-icon sizes.
- The SVG wordmarks use the same modern sans-serif font stack as the product UI. The geometric mark itself is fully path-based and font-independent.

## PNG exports

- Primary and monochrome marks: 512 px.
- Primary and reverse horizontal lockups: 1200 px wide.
- English and Chinese subtitle lockups: 1440 px wide.
- Favicon/application icons: 16, 32, 180, and 512 px.

SVG files are the production masters. PNG files are derived exports and should
be regenerated whenever the master geometry or typography changes.
